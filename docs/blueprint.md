# **App Name**: Evan's Travel

## Core Features:

- Travel Video Showcase: Display a full-width video slider featuring students traveling, airport arrivals, Belarus city views, and university campuses, with smooth fade & zoom animations and overlay text.
- University Directory: Categorize Belarus universities by field of study (Medicine & Health Sciences, IT & Computer Science, Engineering & Technical Sciences, Business, Economics & Management). Each university listing includes a description, photo gallery, and campus/promo videos.
- Visa Service Information: Provide detailed information on Belarus and Dubai visa services, including animated step-by-step timelines for the Belarus student visa process and visa types for Dubai.
- Student & University Media: Showcase student testimonials, arrival & graduation videos, university campus videos, and a student photo gallery with lightbox image viewer and video modal popup.
- Contact Form and Information: Implement a contact form, display the phone number (+375 25 74 58 438), a WhatsApp button, and an animated submit confirmation to facilitate user inquiries.
- Animated Checklists for Required Documents: Visually represent required documents for admission and visa applications using animated checklists to guide users through the application process.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Light blue (#ADD8E6) to convey trust and professionalism, reflecting the agency's international focus.
- Background color: Very light blue (#F0F8FF) to provide a clean and modern backdrop, enhancing readability.
- Accent color: Pale yellow (#FFFFE0) to highlight key information and call-to-action buttons, adding a touch of warmth and optimism.
- Headline font: 'Poppins', sans-serif, for headlines. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Body font: 'PT Sans', sans-serif, for body text. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Use clean, modern icons related to travel, education, and visa services.
- Implement a fully responsive design to ensure the website is accessible and user-friendly on all devices.
- Incorporate subtle animations such as page transitions, scroll-triggered effects, and hover effects to enhance user engagement and create a dynamic browsing experience.
